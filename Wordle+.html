How to play

Guess the word in 6 tries. After each guess, tiles show how close you were.

Green: right letter, right spot.
Yellow: in the word, wrong spot.
Grey: not in the word.

Extras: pick 4, 5 or 6 letters, play Daily or Unlimited, try Hard mode, and use 💡 for one free letter (it costs you nothing but pride).

Guesses must be real words from a built-in dictionary.
